## 🔄 Interaction Flow

### SEO Tab:
```
1. Click "SEO" tab
   ↓
2. Page loads with default values
   ↓
3. Edit any field
   ↓
4. Click "Save SEO Settings"
   ↓
5. Toast notification: "SEO settings updated!"
   ↓
6. Settings saved to localStorage
   ↓
7. Refresh page → Settings persist ✓
```

### Payment Tab:
```
1. Click "Payment" tab
   ↓
2. Page loads with default values
   ↓
3. Toggle Razorpay ON
   ↓
4. Section expands
   ↓
5. Fill in Key ID and Secret
   ↓
6. Click eye icon to verify secret
   ↓
7. Repeat for PhonePe and COD
   ↓
8. Click "Save Payment Settings"
   ↓
9. Toast notification: "Payment settings updated!"
   ↓
10. Check "Active Payment Methods" summary
    ↓
11. Refresh page → Settings persist ✓
```
