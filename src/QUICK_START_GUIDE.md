# 🚀 Sanjari Prints - Quick Start Guide

## For Testing the Complete Application

### 📝 In 5 Simple Steps:

---

## Step 1: Login
Visit the application and click **"Sign In"** in the top navigation bar.

**Demo Credentials (shown on login page):**
- 📧 Email: `demo@sanjariprints.com`
- 🔑 Password: `demo123`

Click **"Sign In"** button → You'll be redirected to your dashboard!

---

## Step 2: View Your Dashboard
After login, you'll see an **advanced dashboard** with:
- Your profile avatar with gradient background and camera icon
- 4 stat cards showing your activity
- **5 powerful tabs:** Profile, Orders, Addresses, Settings, Security

**Try these amazing features:**
- ✏️ Click "Edit Profile" to update your information
- 📸 Hover over avatar to see camera upload button
- 🎁 Check your Loyalty Points card in the sidebar
- 🔍 Use order search and filters
- 📍 Add multiple addresses with labels (Home/Office)
- ⚙️ Toggle notification preferences
- 🔒 Change your password in Security tab

---

## Step 3: Order Something
1. Click **"Browse Products"** button (or use "All Products" in navbar)
2. Choose any category, like **"Documents"** → **"Single Side Printing"**
3. Configure your product:
   - Set pages: 10
   - Set copies: 2
   - Choose paper: A4
   - Upload a file (optional)
4. Click **"Add to Cart"**
5. See the cart icon update with item count! 🛒

---

## Step 4: Complete Checkout
1. Click the **cart icon** (🛒) in navbar
2. Review your items
3. Click **"Proceed to Checkout"**
4. Fill in delivery address (or use sample):
   ```
   Name: John Doe
   Email: john@example.com
   Phone: 9876543210
   Address: 123 Main Street
   City: Mumbai
   State: Maharashtra
   Pincode: 400001
   ```
5. Click **"Continue to Payment"**
6. Select any payment method (UPI, Card, etc.)
7. Click **"Place Order & Pay"**

---

## Step 5: Track Your Order
After placing order:
1. You'll see a success page with your order number (SPR########)
2. Click your **profile avatar** in navbar
3. Select **"My Dashboard"**
4. Go to **"Orders"** tab
5. See your new order with status **"Processing"** 🎉

**Order Details Include:**
- Order number and date
- Status badge (color-coded)
- Items list
- Delivery address
- Payment method
- Total amount
- Estimated delivery date

## ❓ Need Help?

### Common Questions:

**Q: Can I place multiple orders?**
A: Yes! Complete one checkout, then add more items and checkout again.

**Q: Where do I see my order history?**
A: Dashboard → Orders tab (or click profile → My Orders)

**Q: Can I edit my order after placing?**
A: Currently orders cannot be edited, but you can contact support.

**Q: How do I logout?**
A: Click profile avatar → Logout (or use mobile menu)

**Q: Are my orders saved?**
A: Yes, in browser localStorage. They persist across sessions.
